Hero video player
Live YouTube/Clevercast player next to the main heading.
Hero video player —

Shows a live player to the right of the main heading for everyone within about 15 seconds, replacing the hero image while it's live. Paste any normal YouTube link (watch, youtu.be, or /live/) or a Clevercast embed link. Publishing a new link replaces the old one; Turn off removes it until you publish again.

Programme
The list of scheduled sessions, each with an Add to calendar button.
Programme heading & subheading —

Leave both blank to keep the default wording.

Sessions —
No photo

Only Super Admin can add, edit, delete, or reorder sessions. Start and end time are entered and stored as Central European Time (CET) — the site converts them for each visitor's chosen timezone automatically, and generates their "Add to calendar" links from the same instant. Upload a speaker photo to replace the auto-generated initials circle on the site, or leave it blank to keep showing initials. Use the arrows to reorder; sessions show on the site in this order.

Image link cards
A "Useful links" grid of clickable cards you can rename and reposition.
Image link cards —

Shown as a grid of clickable cards between the programme and recordings sections on the site. Each needs an image, title, short body text, and a link. Max 24 cards, images up to 5MB. Use the arrows to reorder.

Text & image section
One content block (title, body text, image) you can reposition, with a choice of layout and image size.
Text & image section —
No image uploaded
45%

Use the toolbar above each field for bold, italic, underline, lists, and links. Choose whether the image sits to the right, to the left, has the text wrapping around it, or sits above the text — and how big it is, as a percentage. Shown as its own section, only while it has a title, body text, or image — leaving everything blank hides it (and always centers whatever's left, regardless of the chosen layout). You can save title/body without an image, or add an image with no text. Use the buttons above to move it: before Sessions, between Sessions and Recordings, or after Recordings (the default).
