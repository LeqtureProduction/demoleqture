Site controls

Enter the admin key to control the survey, the announcement bar, the hero video player, the hero image, the site theme, and the image link cards.

Responses collected —

Turning the survey on makes it appear for visitors, within about 15 seconds. Click refresh to load the new survey responses.

Site announcement —
0/300

Publishing shows this message in a bar at the top of the site for everyone within about 15 seconds, until you clear it or publish something else.

Hero video player —

Shows a live player to the right of the main heading for everyone within about 15 seconds, replacing the hero image while it's live. Paste any normal YouTube link (watch, youtu.be, or /live/) or a Clevercast embed link. Publishing a new link replaces the old one; Turn off removes it until you publish again.

Hero image —
No image uploaded

Shown to the right of the main heading whenever there's no hero video player link published. Max 5MB. Note: while the hero video player is live, this image won't be shown until you turn the player off.

Image link cards —

Shown as a grid of clickable cards between the programme and recordings sections on the site. Each needs an image, title, short body text, and a link. Max 24 cards, images up to 5MB. Use the arrows to reorder.
