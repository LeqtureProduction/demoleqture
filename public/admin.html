Hero video player
Live YouTube/Clevercast player next to the main heading.
Hero video player —

Shows a live player to the right of the main heading for everyone within about 15 seconds, replacing the hero image while it's live. Paste any normal YouTube link (watch, youtu.be, or /live/) or a Clevercast embed link. Publishing a new link replaces the old one; Turn off removes it until you publish again.

Hero
Heading, subheading, and the image or video at the top of the site.
Hero heading & subheading —

Leave both blank to keep the original wording, including the purple "Work Week" highlight — a custom heading replaces the whole line with plain text instead.

Hero image —
No image uploaded

Shown to the right of the main heading whenever there's no hero video player link published. Max 5MB. Note: while the hero video player is live, this image won't be shown until you turn the player off.

📅
Programme
The list of scheduled sessions, each with an Add to calendar button.
Programme heading & subheading —

Leave both blank to keep the default wording.

Sessions —

Only Super Admin can add, edit, delete, or reorder sessions. Start and end time are entered and stored as Central European Time (CET) — the site converts them for each visitor's chosen timezone automatically, and generates their "Add to calendar" links from the same instant. Use the arrows to reorder; sessions show on the site in this order.

Image link cards
A "Useful links" grid of clickable cards you can rename and reposition.
Image link cards —

Shown as a grid of clickable cards between the programme and recordings sections on the site. Each needs an image, title, short body text, and a link. Max 24 cards, images up to 5MB. Use the arrows to reorder.

📝
Text & image section
One content block (title, body text, image) you can reposition.
Text & image section —
No image uploaded

Use the toolbar above each field for bold, italic, underline, lists, and links. Shown as its own section, only while it has a title, body text, or image — leaving everything blank hides it. You can save title/body without an image, or add an image with no text. Use the buttons above to move it: before Sessions, between Sessions and Recordings, or after Recordings (the default).
